# DataCollector Agent — Croatian HNL data inventory

**Research snapshot:** 2026-07-24 (Europe/Zagreb)  
**Scope:** public data suitable for an HNL draft/simulation database, with priority given to HNS/COMET and official club sites. Dynamic values are intentionally described as fields rather than copied as timeless facts.

## Evidence and confidence convention

- **Observed (O):** the field label or value was visible on the cited page/PDF during this review.
- **Inferred (I):** the field is represented by an icon, described by the platform, or is otherwise not exposed as a stable text label in the public HTML. It must be validated before ingestion.
- **Confidence** estimates the reliability of the inventory claim—field availability, grain, and coverage—not whether every individual record is error-free.

HNS itself says that Semafor displays current COMET data, while county associations, competition officers, and clubs participate in entering or refreshing it; missing or erroneous records therefore remain possible. HNS also states that COMET has been used since 2004/05, that public Semafor contains competitions managed through COMET since then, and that pre-2004 competitions are not presently available there; the federation intends to backfill the top division. See the notice on the official [HNS Semafor GNK Dinamo page](https://semafor.hns.family/klubovi/609/gnk-dinamo/).

> **Access gate:** authoritative does not mean freely reusable. The official [HNS Semafor terms](https://hns.family/en/hns/info/terms-of-use-semafor-app/) grant a limited personal, non-commercial license; they prohibit copying/distributing the data without written consent, commercial use, and access by automated systems such as bots or spiders. This document is a small, cited field inventory—not authorization or a production scraping plan. A shipped game or bulk dataset needs HNS’s written permission/licence (and legal review as appropriate).

## Gaps, conflicts, and required handling

1. **No official public OVR exists in HNS Semafor.** Keep `OVR_Rating` explicitly synthetic or source-qualified. FotMob rating and Opta Points are proprietary provider measures, while Transfermarkt market value is an editorial economic estimate; none should silently become “official HNS OVR.”  
   **Confidence: 0.97.**

2. **Assists are not visible in the reviewed HNS competition/player views.** Hajduk profiles expose assists; Transfermarkt, FotMob, Soccerway, and Opta expose or define them with different access reliability. Preserve an `AssistDefinition`/`Source` field because providers can disagree about deflections, own goals, and rebounds. Opta publishes its definition on the [Stats Perform definitions page](https://optaplayerstats.statsperform.com/en_GB/about).  
   **Confidence: 0.95.**

3. **Official totals can still disagree by grain.** HNS season headers may total domestic league plus cup, while the underlying rows separate competitions; club profiles can include league, cup, Europe, and super cup. Never join or compare on `Player + Season` alone—use at minimum `PlayerSourceID + Season + Competition + Club`.  
   **Confidence: 0.97.**

4. **1992–2003/04 is a detailed COMET/player-data gap, not a total official-results gap.** HNS explicitly says pre-2004 competitions cannot currently be served through Semafor and that HNL backfilling is planned. The official [HNL results and tables archive](https://www.hnl.hr/povijest/rezultati-i-poretci/?sid=1) nevertheless publishes round results and final standings from 1992 onward. Transfermarkt/Soccerway or archival publications are still needed for much of the early player-level history, and every such record needs a provenance/confidence flag.  
   **Confidence: 0.99.**

5. **Position/nationality differ by source and time.** HNS’s reviewed player page has birth place but no nationality or detailed position; official club sites and Transfermarkt do. Store a dated player-position record rather than one immutable position, and allow multiple nationalities.  
   **Confidence: 0.96.**

6. **Market value is a dated snapshot.** Do not aggregate values fetched on different dates or infer an actual transfer fee. Use EUR minor units plus valuation date, last-update date, and retrieval timestamp. Missing value must remain null, not zero.  
   **Confidence: 0.98.**

7. **Injuries, fatigue, and substitution cause are not reliably available in the official public pages.** HNS match reports show squad and event timing, while Transfermarkt can label injury/absence status; neither yields a complete physiological fatigue history. The simulation must model these features or use an appropriately licensed availability feed.  
   **Confidence: 0.92.**

8. **Dynamic pages, licence scope, and crawl policy matter.** The [HNS Semafor terms](https://hns.family/en/hns/info/terms-of-use-semafor-app/) limit use to personal/non-commercial purposes and prohibit automated access or copying/distribution without consent. [Transfermarkt’s terms](https://www.transfermarkt.com/intern/anb) prohibit bots, spiders, screen scraping, and automated copying. Hajduk, Soccerway, Opta, and some Transfermarkt routes depend on JavaScript or anti-bot checks. FotMob expressly disallows systematic crawler use on the public site; Opta’s showcase prohibits unlicensed commercial use/distribution. “Publicly viewable” is not equivalent to “authorized for bulk reuse.”  
   **Confidence: 0.98.**

## Reproducible collection procedure

1. Pin `season=2025/26`, competition ID `100391485`, retrieval timestamp, locale, and a run ID before fetching anything.
2. **Only after written permission/licensing**, save the HNS competition responses for each tab, then follow and store canonical club, match, and player IDs. Hash every authorized raw response so later reruns can detect official corrections. Without permission, restrict work to manual, small-scale cited research and do not bulk collect Semafor.
3. Parse standings and fixtures using the Croatian labels first; map into the unified schema only in a derived table. Preserve raw strings, including diacritics, decimal commas, and stoppage-time minutes.
4. Fetch player-season **competition rows**, not only the combined season header. Reconcile `sum(competition)` to the displayed season total and log mismatches.
5. Capture official club roster pages and their player profiles in the same run. Record their visible season labels independently; reject a join if roster season and profile-stat season differ.
6. For a licensed/authorized market-value source, save the valuation date/last-update date as well as retrieval time. Do not automate Transfermarkt’s public pages or retry around anti-bot gates; its terms prohibit screen scraping and automated copying.
7. For Soccerway/Opta/Hajduk rendered pages, archive a screenshot or rendered DOM alongside the extract so icon-only headers can be audited. For FotMob or licensed feeds, use only an authorized acquisition path.
8. Run three reconciliation checks: `(a)` fixture count is 180 league matches for 10 clubs over 36 rounds, `(b)` every club has 36 final league results, and `(c)` player/team goal totals reconcile after own-goal rules. Do not silently impute failed checks.
9. Publish a provenance manifest with source URL, source record ID, retrieved-at time, raw hash, parser version, observed/inferred flag, and confidence for every output table.
